Standing principles

Rules that hold across every step.

01Training and checkpoint selection use only corrected labels. Raw labels are for reporting.
02The model input is the review body and nothing else.
03No decision is ever made with the test set.
04A Macro F1 gap below 0.003 is never called an "advantage".
05B and C train on identical batches; only the sample weights differ.
06If training becomes unstable, LR and batch size are not changed without the project owner's approval.
