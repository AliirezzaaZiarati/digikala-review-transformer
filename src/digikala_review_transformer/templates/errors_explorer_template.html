Hardness score

A correct answer earns 1 ÷ (number of models that got the row right). Solving a row only you can solve is worth 1.0; a row four models solved is worth 0.25.

Margin note — “Correct” alone just mirrors error counts. Lone saves and the hardness score reward the model that wins where others fail.

McNemar test

It looks only at rows where exactly one of the two models was right and asks whether the split is more lopsided than a fair coin. Exact for fewer than 25 disagreements, χ² with continuity correction otherwise.

Margin note — “Both wrong · different guesses” is where the rating band gets interesting: two models failing in two different directions.
